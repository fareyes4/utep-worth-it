Bottom line

How we calculate "pays off"

A degree pays off by earning you more than you'd make without it. We take the median El Paso salary for the field and subtract a $34,000 baseline (typical earnings for a young El Paso worker with only a high-school diploma). That difference is the yearly "wage premium."

The break-even point is the net degree cost (your tuition for the years you choose, minus any financial aid you enter) divided by that yearly premium: the number of years of extra income it takes to cover what the degree cost.
